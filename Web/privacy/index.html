Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy covers Antenna, the TV player for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV published by Code Crave. Antenna collects nothing about you. What you set up in the app stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account, and Code Crave runs no server that receives it.

What Antenna stores

Antenna keeps these in your private iCloud database and syncs them to your devices signed in to the same iCloud account:

  • the sources you add, with their names and addresses, and the text of playlist files you import
  • favorites, profiles, preferences and programme reminders
  • channel groups, hidden groups, and a check value for the group passcode (never the passcode itself)
  • watch progress, My List and recent searches

Your private iCloud database belongs to your Apple Account. Code Crave cannot read it. Apple stores it under its own privacy policy. If a device is not signed in to iCloud, this data stays on that device.

Some things stay on one device only: what you watched last there and other settings for that device, cached posters and guide data, and the notifications for your reminders.

Logins

Usernames, passwords and MAC addresses for your sources are kept in your iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end, and sync to your devices from there. They are never written to the iCloud database or to logs.

There is one exception. When you send a login from your iPhone to an Apple TV, the iPhone seals it with a key that exists only in that Apple TV's memory and places it in your private iCloud database. The Apple TV picks it up and deletes it. It stays there for at most ten minutes.

Network connections

Antenna connects only to:

  • the sources and guide sources you add, and the addresses they list, such as streams, channel logos, posters and guide files
  • Apple, for iCloud sync, the App Store and notifications

Those servers see your IP address and what Antenna asks for, as with any connection, and their own privacy policies apply. If you tap Trailer on a movie, the trailer address your source gives opens outside Antenna.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page here, including the setup page your iPhone opens when Antenna is not installed, GitHub receives the request under its own privacy statement.

Analytics and tracking

Antenna has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and includes no third-party SDK. It does not track you across apps or websites, and Code Crave has no data about you to sell or share.

If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your device's privacy settings, Apple may send Code Crave crash reports and usage statistics from Antenna. Apple controls that setting, and you can turn it off at any time.

Purchases

Apple handles every purchase. Antenna asks the App Store whether you have an active purchase and keeps the answer on the device so the app works offline. Code Crave never sees your name, your Apple Account or your payment details.

Deleting your data

Removing a source in Antenna's settings deletes it, its login, and its favorites, groups and reminders on every device signed in to your iCloud account. Remove your sources before you delete the app, since logins in your iCloud Keychain stay there when the app is deleted.

To delete everything Antenna keeps in iCloud, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage, and delete Antenna's data. Deleting the app removes what it kept on that device.

Children

Antenna is not aimed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included.
