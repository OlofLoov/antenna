The Long Harbor
2024 · 2 h 08 min · Thriller
A harbour pilot uncovers a smuggling ring the night before the port is sold. One tide to decide who gets out. With the buyers arriving at dawn and her own crew split down the middle, she has until the water turns to choose a side, and every channel out of the harbour is watched. Her brother runs the tug that the buyers hired, the harbourmaster owes them money, and the one radio she trusts is on a boat already past the breakwater.
Cast Mira Holt, Jonas Reeve, Adaeze Okafor, Teodor Lind, Ines Barros, Paul Achterberg, Kaito Mori
Director Sanna Weiss
