What is a source?
Antenna includes no channels. A source is the playlist or login a TV provider gives you.
M3U playlist
A link ending in .m3u or .m3u8. It lists every channel with its stream address. A second link, in XMLTV format, carries the programme guide and is optional.
Xtream login
A server address with a username and password, sometimes called Xtream Codes. The server answers with channels, guide, movies and series, so there is nothing else to add. The login is saved in the Keychain on this Apple TV.
