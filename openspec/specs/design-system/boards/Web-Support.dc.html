Questions
Does Antuna include channels?
No. Antuna plays sources you already have: a playlist link or file, an Xtream Codes login, a Stalker portal, or a Jellyfin or Channels DVR server.
A channel will not play
The message on screen says why: the source is offline, refused the login, or sent a format the device cannot decode. Try another channel from the same source first.
The guide says No programme information
Open the source in Settings and check its guides. You can pick the right guide entry for a channel by hand.
How do I unlock Antuna on a new device?
Sign in with the same Apple Account and it unlocks on its own. If it does not, use Restore Purchases on the paywall.
How do I cancel the yearly subscription?
In your Apple Account's subscription settings, at least 24 hours before it renews.
