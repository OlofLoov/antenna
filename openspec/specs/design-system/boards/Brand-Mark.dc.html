The mark
One set of shapes from the owner's artwork, in SVG user units with the baseline at y 0. Letters are 38 wide with round caps. The play triangle and the mouth are cut through, so the ground shows (design-system/the-antuna-mark, design-system/the-mark-s-colors). The still mark has no eye.
App icon
The a as in the icon artwork: translate(120 134) scale(1.05) translate(-675 59) on a 240 tile, radius 54. The standard icon has the glow, a radial gradient from #1A2A44 at (120, 60), radius 170, to #0B0E13; in the dark and tinted appearances the glow is hidden. Alternates swap the tips (and for Light the ink and ground); Teal is the standard icon now, so its alternate is Blue (app-release/alternate-icons-keep-the-mark).
Apple TV icon and Top Shelf
Three layers, so the antennae, the a and the ground shift apart on focus (app-release/every-app-shows-its-icon).
Motion
Frames from the three motions; the owner's prototype is the reference and design.md decision 2 has every track. Played by BrandMark.Word or BrandMark.Letter with a MarkMoment; the accessibility value is the moment (hello, tuning, gotIt, still).
Reduce Motion
With Reduce Motion on nothing draws, leans, hops or slides (design-system/reduce-motion-stills-the-mark). Hello fades the still mark in over 0.4 s. Got it fades it from 0.5 to 1 over 0.4 s. Tuning pulses the tips' opacity 1, 0.35, 1 every 2.4 s.
