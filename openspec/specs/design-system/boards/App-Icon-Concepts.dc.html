A. Mast and wavesRecommended
A mast with a node and two waves on each side. The same idea as the radio-waves mark the app already shows on the tab bar, the sidebar, onboarding and the paywall, drawn fresh so no SF Symbol ships in the icon. At 16 px the node and the inner waves still read. On Apple TV it splits into ground, waves and mast.

B. Play in waves
A rounded play triangle sending two waves to the right. Says video and broadcast at once, but a play triangle is the mark of most video apps, and next to the waves it reads as a speaker volume symbol.

Apple TV, concept A
tvOS draws its icon from a layered image stack: 400 by 240 pt on the Home Screen (shown at 1x), 1280 by 768 px for the App Store. The system moves the layers apart under the Siri Remote, so the waves sit behind the mast. No glass on Apple TV; the layers are flat PNGs.
