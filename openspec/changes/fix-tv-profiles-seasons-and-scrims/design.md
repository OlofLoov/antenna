# Design

## Boards and design-system requirements

Boards: `TV-Profiles`, `iPhone-Profiles`, `TV-Series`, `iPhone-Series`, `iPad-Series`, `TV-Movies`, `TV-Movies-Trailer`, `TV-Movies-Poster-Only`, `TV-Movies-Preview`, `TV-Movies-Preview-Loading`, `TV-Movies-Preview-Poster-Only`, `TV-Hints-Movies`, `TV-Title`, `TV-Title-Cast`, `TV-Title-Scores`, `iPad-Movies`, `iPad-Movies-Trailer`, `iPad-Movies-Poster-Only`, `iPad-Title`, `iPad-Title-Scores`, `iPhone-Movies`, `iPhone-Movies-Trailer`, `iPhone-Movies-Grid`.

Requirements followed: `design-system/focus-is-the-lifted-white-card`, `design-system/apple-tv-content-stays-in-the-safe-area` (room for the focused item's growth), `design-system/text-meets-contrast-minimums` (0.72 or more under the copy), `design-system/layouts-mirror-in-right-to-left-languages`, `design-system/visual-changes-update-the-boards-in-the-same-change`.

## Why each bug came back or stayed

- Profile circles. `0ef14586` drew the circle with its own ring and scale inside a plain button style, and nothing cut it. `3b99b09e` (refresh-visual-design) swapped that style for `.borderless` and put the highlight hover effect on the circle. On Apple TV a borderless button lifts its label on a rounded platter cut to the label's frame, so the circle got two lifts and the outer one cut the inner one into a rounded square. Moving the effect to the circle alone does not help: on tvOS the hover effects ignore the circular hover shape (see Decisions). No test looked at what is drawn around the focused tile. `f41e6cb5` (adopt-tvos-tab-view) moved the profile button from the tab bar to the top of Settings as `ProfileSwitchButton` and gave it the same `.borderless` style and circular highlight, so the cut came back there.
- Season chips. `4d292dc3` turned clipping off on the chip row so a focused chip's lift would not be cut. That lets chips past the row's end draw over the count beside it. Every fixture had three or four seasons, which never reach the count, so nothing showed it until a real series with many seasons.
- Hero scrim. Never fixed in code. `11808b25` built the banner from the boards, which draw a flat 0.72 block 1100 pt (Apple TV) or 560 pt (iPad) wide; the block's edge was there from the start. `d0cbb5bd`, `b208dbe7`, `b5fe772c` and `fix-browse-art-text-and-speed` fixed the picture under it (cut, stretched, posters shown whole at the right), which made the edge easier to see: a poster at the right over its blurred copy sits right where the block stops. `4581d0e8` copied the same block into the Apple TV preview. The Apple TV and iPad title pages used their own gradient that does not mirror in Arabic. The root cause is the boards and the per-view copies, so this change fixes both: the boards draw one gradient, one component draws it, and a rule check fails on a flat block over art.

## Decisions

- **Profile tile focus.** The tile button takes `FocusHolderStyle`, the existing style for a button that shows focus itself, and on Apple TV the focused circle grows by a tenth and casts the focus shadow, while the name turns bold and white. Tried on the tvOS 27.2 simulator: `.borderless` and `.highlight` and `.lift` hover effects each draw a rounded rectangle platter around the circle, with or without `contentShape(.hoverEffect, Circle())`, and `.highlight` cuts the lifted circle to it. `.card` lifts a rectangle too. Deviation, named under `design-system/deviations-from-a-design-rule-are-named`: the circle has no system tilt and no platter, against `design-system/focus-is-the-lifted-white-card`, because the system effect cannot draw a circle. Focus still shows by more than size: the shadow and the name's weight and color change with it.
- **Profile button in Settings.** `ProfileSwitchButton` takes `FocusHolderStyle` and the same `circleLiftOnTelevision` as the tiles. `scripts/check-rules.sh` fails on `contentShape(.hoverEffect, Circle())` in `Apps/Shared`, the pattern both regressions used.
- **Season row.** Clip the chip row to itself and give it 24 pt above and below on Apple TV (6 elsewhere, as before) for the lift and shadow. The count stays at the trailing edge, as the boards draw it. Options: wrap the chips onto more lines (fifteen seasons take four lines on iPhone and push the episodes down) or move the count above the row (departs from every Series board).
- **Hero scrim.** `HeroScrim(copyEnd:)`: a gradient from 0.85 at the leading edge to 0.72 where the copy ends, 0.3 halfway to the trailing edge, 0 at it, flipped in right-to-left. `copyEnd` is 0.5 on the Apple TV banner and preview (copy to x 944 of 1920), 0.57 on the Apple TV title page, 0.56 on the iPad banner (copy to 508 of 914) and 0.6 on the iPad title card. The iPhone banner keeps its band at the foot, as `HeroFootScrim`: clear to 0.78 over the 56 pt above the copy, then 0.78 to 0.86. Options: a full-width flat dim (darkens the whole picture), a material (blurs art the boards want sharp).
- **Fixture.** `-ui-testing-many-seasons` gives Northern Lights fifteen seasons of two episodes, ids `xtream#cabin#episode#1011` to `#1152`. A launch argument instead of a new series, so no existing row, banner or search test changes.

## Tests

- `HeroScrimTests` renders the scrim and the Apple TV, iPad and iPhone banners with `ImageRenderer` and fails on any step in brightness across the picture, and on the dark side not following the reading direction.
- `ShellUITests.testTheFocusedCircleShowsWhole` and `testTheFocusedProfileButtonShowsWhole` (Apple TV) read the screenshot around the focused circle: the circle is as tall as it is wide, and past it there is only the ground color.
- `TitleUITests` and `TitleTVUITests.testFifteenSeasonsScrollClearOfTheCount` read the screenshot inside the count's frame before and after scrolling to Season 15: most of it must be the ground color.
- Previews: fifteen seasons on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV; the hero scrim in English and Arabic.
- `scripts/check-rules.sh` fails on `Palette.ground.opacity(…).frame(width:` and the like in `Apps/Shared`.
